French Press Brew Guide

Grind and Boil Bloom and Plunge
Blooming Grinds

Watch as we bloom the grind, letting air escape the grounds before finishing the pour. Wait 30 seconds before finishing filling the press (depending on amound brewed).

Plunging

Watch as we plunge the plunger slowly. The plunge should take about 30 seconds (depending on amount of coffee brewed).
