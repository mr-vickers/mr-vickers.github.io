For excellent French-press coffee, follow these steps.

Step one - Pour grounds into brewer

Note: Use ~1 gram of coffee grounds per 15 grams of water (ratio of 1:15).

Step two - Bring water to a near boil

Note: Do not exceed 205 degrees Fahrenheit (≈96 °C).

Step three - Pour some water to let the grounds bloom

Bloom for ~30 seconds to release trapped air pockets.

Step four - Wait four minutes

Click Start to begin a 4-minute brew timer.

Use this timer to track your brewing phase. You're aiming for four minutes.

Step five - Slowly plunge

Plunge slowly over ~30 seconds with steady pressure (adjust for brew size).
